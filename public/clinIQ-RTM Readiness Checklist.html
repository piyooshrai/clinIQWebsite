5 Things That Determine Whether RTM Generates Revenue or Just Creates More Admin Work
Before you enroll your first patient, these five infrastructure decisions will determine everything. Get them right, and RTM becomes a compounding revenue line. Get them wrong, and it becomes a compliance burden that costs more than it generates.
1
👥
Eligible Patient Volume
  • Qualifying conditions: MSK, respiratory, cognitive behavioral, medication adherence
  • Minimum viable cohort: 50+ eligible patients to justify program setup overhead
  • Quick filter: chronic MSK conditions, COPD, asthma, post-surgical rehab patients
  • Run a panel audit before launch — most clinics underestimate eligible volume by 30–40%
2
📡
Device & Platform Selection
  • FDA-cleared devices required for all RTM billing — non-cleared devices void claims
  • Must integrate with your EHR for seamless, auditable data flow
  • Patient compliance rates vary 40–80% depending on device complexity
  • clinIQ supports 20+ device types with auto-enrollment workflows built in
3
💰
Billing Infrastructure
  • 98975 setup $22.00   98976 respiratory $52.00   98977 MSK $51.44
  • 98980 treatment mgmt first 20 min $52.23   98981 each add'l $41–$42.42
  • 98985 / 98979 2026 NEW   2–15 day periods — expands billable patients by 20–40%
  • Separate RTM revenue tracking from standard E/M billing is essential for ROI visibility
4
⚙️
Staff Workflow Design
  • Define ownership: who monitors device alerts? Clinical vs. admin split must be explicit
  • Assign monthly check-ins: who conducts the 20-min patient management call?
  • Without automation: 45 min/patient/month — unsustainable at scale
  • With clinIQ automation: 8 min/patient/month — 82% reduction in staff overhead
5
🔍
Payer Coverage Verification
  • Medicare covers all RTM codes nationally — no payer-level verification needed
  • Commercial payer coverage varies significantly by state, plan, and contract year
  • Verify your top 5 payers before launch — coverage gaps sink programs mid-cycle
  • Pre-auth requirements differ per payer — automated verification saves 15–45 min per case
